Foam Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts board feet with multiplication and division: the board feet a job takes at a thickness, the whole kits and what is left in the last one, the area one kit covers at a thickness, and the cost per board foot. The area, the thickness, the kit yield and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own kit box. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your cavities and does not know your overspray, your pass thickness limits, your temperature window or your local foam rules, and it gives no verdict that a thickness performs. Every number here is your own entry.

The board-feet truth

Board feet: the area times the inches of thickness.

The kits truth

Whole kits: rounded up, and the board feet left in the last one.

The coverage truth

Coverage: one kit's board feet spread at your thickness.

The cost truth

Cost: the kits at their price, and what that is per board foot.
